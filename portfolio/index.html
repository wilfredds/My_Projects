~/about

I am a fourth-year BS Information Technology student at Lyceum of the Philippines University, Cavite, graduating in 2027. I lead the team behind our capstone, FloodGuard. Most of what I know came from building things that had to work for somebody other than me.

AutoCare exists because a car care shop was writing every job in a notebook and losing money to arithmetic done at the counter. RallyReady exists because training badminton alone means nobody is there to call the corners. Bike Guide PH exists because beginner riders here have nowhere local to learn gearing.

I work across the stack: Next.js and Prisma on Postgres, React and TypeScript on Supabase, Flutter and Riverpod on Firebase. The parts I care most about are the ones that never demo well. Database constraints that make bad data impossible. Security rules with assertions behind them. A pipeline that goes red before a user finds the bug.

Every project on this page sits in one repository with its own workflow. None of it is a tutorial clone.

autocare/

A job-management system for a car care shop, built to replace a paper notebook. Recording a job takes seven short screens with one decision on each. The running total is computed by the server and there is no field to type it into. Every finished job prints a receipt. The whole interface works in English or Filipino, and every change is written to an activity log.

React · TypeScript · Supabase

rallyready/

Badminton training for a player with no coach and no partner. Every corner call is spoken out loud, so you never have to look at the screen. It times your split-step, throws in audible fake calls, generates periodised programs, and tracks how much load you are carrying so it can tell you to back off. Installs to a phone, and the trainer runs with no connection.

  • React 19

bike-guide-app/

A cycling companion for Filipino beginners. The gear simulator shows which ring and cog to use on a climb and warns you when the chain is crossed.
