Self-directed badminton training for a player with no coach and no partner. The app is the random caller. Every corner is spoken out loud, so a whole session can go by without you looking at the screen once.

$cat problem.md

Badminton footwork gets trained by someone shouting a corner at you. Alone, there is nobody to shout, no partner to feed, and often no court. What is left is a dozen YouTube tabs and no record of whether any of it worked.

$cat solution.md

  • Audio first. Every call is spoken, carries its own tone and buzzes the phone. You cannot watch a screen and move to a corner at the same time, so the screen is optional.
  • Split-step training. An optional metronome tick fires a configurable 0.2 to 0.7 seconds before each call, so you learn to land the split-step as the call arrives. That timing is the most common club-player fault I know of.
  • Deception that actually deceives. A fake call, then the real one, forcing a second split-step. The fake sounds identical to the real thing, because a fake you can hear coming trains nothing.
  • Load instead of minutes. One tap rates effort from 1 to 10. Effort multiplied by minutes is the number that compares a brutal ten minutes against an easy half hour, and the app watches the ramp. Training alone means nobody else is watching for the overuse injury.
  • It bends to the day. Three taps for sleep, legs and energy, and the session gets lighter or heavier. A written plan cannot tell that you slept four hours.
  • Periodised programs. Four to sixteen week plans built from four choices, moving through Base, Build and Sharpen, with a deload every fourth week and a taper at the end. Today's session is the first card on the home screen.

$cat engineering.md

There are 341 tests under Vitest, and npm run verify (typecheck, lint, tests, build) is what CI runs on every push. The drill engine, the load model, the program generator and the benchmark scoring are all pure functions with tests behind them, because those are the parts deciding how hard somebody trains.

It installs as a PWA. The timer, the drills and the coaching notes work with no connection at all, sessions still log, and everything syncs to Supabase once the network comes back. Auth, progress and published programs live in Postgres behind row-level security.

The original brief asked for curated third-party video and I left it out. Embedding other people's coaching clips means shipping a library I do not own and cannot keep alive. The reference library is written instead. I wrote that decision down in the repo rather than quietly dropping the feature.

$cat why-it-matters.md

It is the most complete thing I have built. Five phases, a real domain model, an offline story, an audio pipeline, and enough tests that I can change the program generator on a Tuesday without being afraid of it.
